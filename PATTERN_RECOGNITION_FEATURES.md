# 🔮 Advanced Pattern Recognition & Prediction Features

## ✅ **Pattern Detection Capabilities:**

### 1. **BREAKOUT PATTERNS** 🚀
- **Bullish Breakout**: Price breaks above resistance with 2x volume
- **Bearish Breakdown**: Price breaks below support with high volume
- **Confidence**: 70-90% when volume confirms the move
- **Prediction**: Next price targets based on breakout level

### 2. **CONSOLIDATION PATTERNS** 📊
- **Detection**: Price moving in tight 5% range
- **Signal**: Indicates upcoming major move (up or down)
- **Prediction**: Breakout direction based on trend context

### 3. **TREND REVERSAL PATTERNS** 🔄
- **RSI Divergence**: RSI >80 (extreme overbought) or <20 (oversold)
- **Volume Confirmation**: Unusual volume at reversal points
- **Prediction**: Direction and probability of reversal

### 4. **MOMENTUM PATTERNS** ⚡
- **Strong Momentum**: Price >15% away from 20-day moving average
- **Acceleration**: Rate of price change increasing
- **Prediction**: Continuation vs exhaustion signals

### 5. **SUPPORT/RESISTANCE TESTS** 🎯
- **Key Level Tests**: Price within 2% of major S/R levels
- **Bounce vs Break**: Volume and RSI help predict outcome
- **Prediction**: Probability of level holding or breaking
