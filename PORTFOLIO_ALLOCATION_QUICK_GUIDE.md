# 📊 Portfolio Allocation Sheet - Quick Action Guide

## 🎯 What This Sheet Tells You

Your Portfolio Allocation sheet is a **complete action plan** for your entire portfolio. Each row is one stock with specific instructions on what to do.

---

## 🔑 Key Columns Explained (in Plain English)

### **ACTION Column** - Your Main Decision
| Value | What to Do |
|-------|-----------|
| **KEEP** | ✅ Hold this stock - it's good quality |
| **BUY** | 💰 Invest more money in this stock |
| **SELL** | ❌ Exit this position (sell all shares) |
| **HOLD** | ⏸️ Keep for now, but watch closely |

### **WHEN_TO_ACT Column** - Timing
| Value | When to Execute |
|-------|-----------------|
| **TODAY** | 🔴 Take action today |
| **THIS WEEK** | 🟡 Within next 5 trading days |
| **THIS MONTH** | 🟢 Within next 30 days |
| **MONITOR** | 👀 Just watch, no urgent action |

### **INVEST_₹ Column** - Money to Add
- **₹0** = Don't invest more
- **₹5,000+** = Add this much capital to this stock

### **BUY_SHARES Column** - Quantity to Buy
- **0** = Don't buy more shares
- **10+** = Buy this many shares

### **MY_PROFIT_% Column** - Your Gain/Loss
- **Positive (+10%)** = 📈 You're making profit
- **Negative (-5%)** = 📉 You're in loss
- **0%** = Break-even

### **BOOK_%_IF_SELL Column** - Profit Booking
- **0%** = Keep entire position
- **30-50%** = Sell 30-50% of shares, keep rest
- **100%** = Sell everything

### **SCORE Column** - Quality Rating (0-100)
- **80-100** = 🌟 Excellent quality
- **70-79** = ✅ Good quality
- **60-69** = 👍 Decent quality
- **50-59** = ⚠️ Below average
- **<50** = 🔴 Poor quality
