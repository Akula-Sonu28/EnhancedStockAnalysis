# Rakesh Jhunjhunwala — Investment Framework (India)

## Core philosophy

**Bet on India.** Back **scalable businesses** with **entrepreneurial management** and hold with conviction through volatility. "Markets will reward patience, but punish complacency without research."

## How he chose stocks

### Macro belief
- **India GDP and consumption** multi-decade growth story
- **Financialization** — banks, NBFCs, asset managers as enablers
- Equity culture deepening → liquidity and rerating over time

### Stock selection filters
| Filter | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Growth runway** | Revenue/profit can compound 5–10+ years |
| **Management** | Promoter/operators with skin in game, capital allocation sense |
| **Scalable model** | Not one-off project businesses |
| **Entry valuation** | Willing to pay for quality; not pure deep value |
| **Conviction sizing** | Few large positions (Titan, Lupin, CRISIL-era examples) |

### Sectors he favored (historical pattern)
- **Consumer & retail** (Titan, Metro Brands themes)
- **Financials** (RBL, YES past; broader banking thesis)
- **Pharma/API** selective
- **Hotels, media** when cycle + growth aligned
- **Avoid:** weak governance regardless of story

## Trends he rode

- **India's middle class** consumption upgrade
- **Credit growth** cycles in financials
- **Market rerating** of Indian equities vs historical discount
- **IPO/listing winners** when business quality clear (with caution on hype)

## How he made money

- **Long-only equity** compounding + occasional trading around core
- **Concentration** in best convictions
- **Leverage selectively** (controversial — increased risk; educational note only)
- **Public bullishness** on India — psychological alignment with long book
- **Partnership with operators** — board/investor relationships

## Red flags (Jhunjhunwala would avoid)

- Promoter **pledge** stress, opaque related parties
- **Accounting surprises** repeated
- Businesses without **cash flow** backing growth
- Pure **tip-driven** trades without thesis
