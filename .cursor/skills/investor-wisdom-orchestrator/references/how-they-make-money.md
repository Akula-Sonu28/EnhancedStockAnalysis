# How Famous Investors Make Money

A comparative reference for the investor-wisdom skill set. Money is made from **edge × position size × time × psychological discipline** — not from copying tickers.

## The four edges

| Edge type | Who uses it | How money is made |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Analytical** | Graham, Greenblatt | Buy mispriced vs intrinsic value; mean reversion |
| **Informational** | Fisher (scuttlebutt), Lynch (channel checks) | Know business quality before market prices it in |
| **Structural** | Buffett (float, permanence) | Cheap permanent capital + compounding |
| **Macro/reflexive** | Soros, Druckenmiller, Dalio | Bet on policy/flows; size up when thesis confirms |
| **Cycle timing** | Marks, Templeton | Buy fear, sell euphoria; avoid permanent loss |
| **Thematic growth** | Lynch, Wood, Jhunjhunwala | Ride S-curves; sell when growth decelerates or thesis breaks |

## Selection basis (what they look at first)

| Investor | Primary filter | Secondary filter | Ignore / deprioritize |
|----------|----------------|------------------|------------------------|
| Graham | Net-net, NCAV, margin of safety | Earnings stability | Quality of management (somewhat) |
| Buffett | Durable moat, ROIC, owner earnings | Honest capable management | Short-term earnings noise |
| Munger | Quality + rationality | Inversion (what kills this?) | Complexity outside circle |
| Lynch | Earnings growth vs PE (PEG) | Understandable story | Macro unless extreme |
| Fisher | Sales growth durability | Management integrity (scuttlebutt) | Quarterly volatility |
| Greenblatt | High ROIC + high earnings yield | Special sits | Glamour narratives |
| Dalio | Macro regime fit | Risk parity balance | Single-stock idolatry |
| Marks | Price vs intrinsic under cycle | Risk of permanent loss | “This time is different” |
| Druckenmiller | Asymmetric macro + liquidity | Growth winners when sure | Diversification for its own sake |
| Soros | Reflexive trend + policy error | Position scaling | Being right without sizing |
| Wood | Disruptive platform + Wright’s Law | TAM expansion | Current profitability alone |
| Jhunjhunwala | India growth + scalable model | Management skin in game | Short-term index noise |
| Damani | Unit economics, reinvestment runway | Low competition retail/format | Hot tips |
| Kedia | SMILE attributes in small/mid | Scalable niche leadership | Large-cap comfort only |
| Agrawal | QGLP — quality of earnings | Long runway sectors | Cheap junk |

## Trends they ride (examples)

| Trend theme | Typical champions | How they monetize |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Consumer brands / staples | Buffett, Damani | Pricing power + reinvestment |
| Financials / credit cycles | Buffett, Jhunjhunwala, Marks | Underwrite risk when spreads wide |
| Tech / innovation | Lynch, Fisher, Wood | Early adoption → scale |
| Commodity / cyclicals | Marks, Druckenmiller | Buy distressed at cycle trough |
| Rates / FX / liquidity | Dalio, Soros, Druckenmiller | Macro instruments + equity tilt |
| India demographics | Jhunjhunwala, Kedia, Agrawal | Long-only equity in structural winners |
| Special situations | Graham, Greenblatt | Corporate events, spinoffs, mislabels |

## Position sizing & holding period

| Style | Sizing | Hold | Exit trigger |
|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| Deep value | Many small bets | Until re-rated or impaired | Thesis break, better opportunity |
| Quality compounder | Few large concentrated | Decades | Moat erosion, capital misallocation |
| GARP | 20–40 names | 1–5 years | PE outruns growth, story changes |
| Macro | Few huge when convicted | Days–months | Thesis invalidated, stop on reflexivity |
| Cycle | Scale in/out by sentiment | Full cycle | Euphoria / forced selling reversed |

## Common failure modes (all styles)

1. **Style drift** — value investor chasing momentum junk
2. **Overconfidence after wins** — Druckenmiller: “It’s not whether you’re right, it’s how much you make when right”
3. **Illiquidity trap** — correct but cannot hold (leverage, redemptions)
4. **Narrative without numbers** — Wood-style disruption without unit economics
5. **Ignoring regime** — quality stocks crushed in liquidity crises (Marks)
6. **India-specific:** promoter governance, related-party risk, accounting (apply to all Indian names)

## NSE application note

When applying any framework to Indian stocks:

- Check **promoter holding, pledge, related-party transactions**
- Prefer **consolidated** financials; watch **Ind AS** adjustments
- **Liquidity** matters more in small/mid (Kedia, Veliyath style)
- **Regulatory** shifts (RBI, SEBI, sector policy) hit macro-style investors faster

These skills do **not** use Stock_Analysis v1/v2 scoring — they are independent philosophy lenses.
