# Cathie Wood — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

Invest in **disruptive innovation** — technologies replacing old solutions via **rapid cost decline** (Wright's Law) and **exponential adoption**. Five-year+ horizon; high volatility accepted.

## ARK innovation platforms

| Platform | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **AI & automation** | ML platforms, robotics, autonomous |
| **Energy storage** | Batteries, EV supply chain |
| **Genomic revolution** | CRISPR, molecular diagnostics |
| **Fintech** | Digital wallets, blockchain infrastructure |
| **Next-gen internet** | Cloud, streaming, digital platforms |

## How she chooses stocks

### TAM / adoption curve
- **Total addressable market** expanding as costs fall
- **S-curve** — early inflection = maximum growth phase
- **Convergence** — AI + genomics + cloud enabling new models

### Wright's Law
- Each **doubling of cumulative production** → predictable cost drop
- Enables mass market penetration (solar, batteries historical examples)

### Financial lens (secondary to vision)
- Reinvest aggressively for **share capture**
- Tolerates low/negative near-term profits if **unit economics** improve on curve
- **Management vision** and R&D intensity

## Trends Wood emphasizes

- **AI compute and software** eating industries
- **EV disruption** of ICE autos
- **Digital finance** bypassing legacy banks
- **Precision medicine**

## How she makes money (when successful)

- **Early capture** of exponential revenue growth
- **Portfolio of options** — few huge winners pay for many failures
- **Volatility harvesting** — ADD on conviction during drawdowns (controversial)
- **Research marketing** — inflows enable scale in themes

## Risks & skeptic counterbalance (include in analysis)

- **Duration risk** — rising rates hurt long-dated cash flows
- **Execution risk** — TAM ≠ market share
- **Competition** — incumbents and other disruptors
- **Concentration** — thematic overlap amplifies drawdowns
- **Narrative without cash flow** — bubble phase possible

- India proxies: **IT services AI pivot**, fintech (Paytm-era caution), renewable supply chain, pharma CRAMS
