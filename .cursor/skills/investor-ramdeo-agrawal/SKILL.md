# Ramdeo Agrawal — Investment Framework (India)

## Core philosophy

**QGLP** — invest in **quality** businesses with **growth**, **longevity** of opportunity, at a fair **price**. Structural **megatrends** in India (financialization, consumption, formalization) create decade-long equity wealth.

## QGLP breakdown

| Pillar | Questions |
|--------|-----------|
| **Quality** | ROCE, cash flows, governance, competitive position |
| **Growth** | Revenue/ profit CAGR sustainable? |
| **Longevity** | Will opportunity last 10–20 years? |
| **Price** | Valuation reasonable vs growth and quality? |

**All four required** — quality alone at wrong price fails; cheap without longevity is value trap.

## How he chooses stocks

### Structural themes (MOFSL research tradition)
- **Financial services** depth — banks, AMCs, insurance
- **Consumption** upgrade — brands, discretionary
- **Industrial & capital goods** — India capex cycles
- **Export / global competitiveness** — manufacturing scale
- **Technology enablers** — IT, platforms

### Financial discipline
- **ROCE > cost of capital** through cycles
- **Free cash flow** conversion
- **Low leverage** preferred
- **Promoter quality** and capital allocation

### Portfolio approach
- **Core-satellite** — quality compounders core
- **Long holding periods** aligned with structural themes
- **Avoid excessive churn** and macro timing

## Trends Agrawal emphasizes

- **India GDP** → **corporate profit** pool expansion (historical under-index)
- **Formalization** post-GST/digitization
- **Financial savings** shift to equities/mutual funds
- **Demographic dividend** — consumption and housing

## How he makes money

- **Thematic compounding** — ride structural winners
- **Research edge** — institutional depth applied to long horizons
- **Discipline on price** — even great stories need entry sanity
- **Wealth via time** — "Equity is a long-term asset class"

## What Agrawal avoids

- **Short-term trading** as primary strategy
- **Low quality** cheap stocks (Q without GLP)
- **Overpaying** for growth without moat (P too high)
- **Thematic overload** without financial proof
