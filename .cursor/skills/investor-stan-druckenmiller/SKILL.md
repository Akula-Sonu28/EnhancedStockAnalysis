# Stan Druckenmiller — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

**Preserve capital first.** Make **large, concentrated bets** when you have high conviction — especially macro asymmetry. "It's not whether you're right or wrong, but how much money you make when you're right."

## How he chooses investments

### Top-down macro (primary edge)
- **Central bank liquidity** — "Don't fight the Fed" (and global CBs)
- **Currency trends** — USD cycle drives EM/risk assets
- **Inflation / growth surprises** — reposition fast
- **Thesis must have asymmetric payoff** — small loss if wrong, huge gain if right

### Bottom-up growth (when macro aligns)
- Best **growth businesses** in favorable liquidity — concentrated
- Will pay higher multiples if **earnings trajectory** explosive
- Example historical: early internet/tech winners with macro tailwind

### Process
1. Form macro view (liquidity, policy)
2. Find **expression** (indices, sectors, currencies, best stocks)
3. **Size up** when trade works — pyramiding winners
4. **Cut quickly** when wrong — no ego

## Trends he has traded

- **Fed easing/hiking cycles** — rates, dollar, equities
- **EM crises** — currency breaks, rebound trades
- **Tech/growth supercycles** when liquidity abundant
- **Commodity spikes** with inflation shocks

## How he makes money

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Concentration** | Best ideas dominate portfolio |
| **Liquidity timing** | Risk-on when CBs ease |
| **Asymmetry** | Risk 1 to make 5–10 |
| **Flexibility** | No mandate drift — go where opportunity is |
| **No benchmark hugging** | Absolute return focus |

## Position sizing rules (conceptual)

- Low conviction: small or zero
- High conviction + macro tailwind: **very large**
- When wrong: exit — survival enables next bet

## What he avoids

- **Hedging away** a high-conviction view (historically)
- Slow death of small losses adding up without cutting
- Investing without liquidity/regime awareness
- Diversification that dilutes edge

## India application

- Track **RBI vs Fed**, INR, FPI flows, liquidity (LAF, G-SAP)
- In risk-on: leading **Nifty growth** / financials beta
- In risk-off: reduce beta; don't fight liquidity withdrawal
- Stock level: scalable growth + macro tailwind (consumption credit cycle)
