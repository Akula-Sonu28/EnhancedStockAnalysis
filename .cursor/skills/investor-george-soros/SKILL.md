# George Soros — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

**Reflexivity:** Market participants' **biased views affect fundamentals**, which feed back into prices — trends can overshoot equilibrium. Profits come from recognizing **self-reinforcing phases** and inflection points.

## Reflexivity loop

```
Bias → Price move → Affects fundamentals (credit, investment, sentiment)
     → Reinforces bias → Boom OR Bust
Eventually: divergence from reality → reversal
```

## How he chooses trades

### Macro thesis first
- **Currency regimes** (EMS, GBP 1992, Asian crisis)
- **Credit bubbles** — reflexive lending inflates assets
- **Policy error** — central bank behind the curve

### Test thesis
- Look for **confirming price action** — scale in when working
- **Stop/reverse** when thesis breaks — survival critical

### Equities
- Often expression of macro view (indices, country baskets)
- **Not** long-only quality — **bet on trend + inflection**

## Trends Soros traded

- **Sterling / EMS** — forced devaluation bets
- **Asian financial crisis** — reflexive credit unwind
- **Tech bubble** — participate rise, recognize unsustainability
- **Open society / geopolitical** themes affecting capital flows

## How he makes money

- **Asymmetric macro** — small loss, huge win (Druckenmiller similar)
- **Speed of recognition** at inflection
- **Scale** — size when reflexivity confirms
- **Philanthropy/geopolitical** network informing flows (controversial edge)

## Risk management

- **Survive first** — cut losing macro views
- **No dogma** — "I'm only rich because I know when I'm wrong"
- **Generalized reflexivity** — apply to bubbles (crypto, SPACs, etc.)

## India application

- **INR / RBI / Fed** reflexivity on FPI flows
- **NBFC credit boom-bust** — reflexive lending to developers/consumers
- **Policy reform waves** — bullish reflexivity until implementation lag hits
- Stock picking secondary to **regime bet**
