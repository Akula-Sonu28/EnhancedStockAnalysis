# Radhakishan Damani — Investment Framework (India)

## Core philosophy

**Observe quietly, act rarely, hold decades.** Master **consumer/retail unit economics** and **compounding through reinvestment**. Extreme privacy — thesis built from ground-level observation, not media.

## How he chooses stocks

### Business model focus
- **High-frequency essential consumption** (DMart: grocery, value retail)
- **Low gross margins but high turns** → ROCE through velocity
- **Real estate / store rollout** as moat when executed cheaply
- **Pricing discipline** — EDLP (everyday low price) wins trust

### Selection traits
| Trait | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Scalable format** | Replication city to city |
| **Cost leadership** | Cannot be undercut easily |
| **Cash generative** | Funds expansion without dilution |
| **Patient capital** | Ignore quarterly noise |
| **Simple accounting** | Understand every line |

### Portfolio style (known pattern)
- **Concentrated** core (DMart/Avenue Supermarts)
- **Strategic stakes** in banks, hospitality (India Cements, etc.)
- **Very low portfolio turnover**
- **Avoids** publicity and tips culture

## Trends he rode

- **Organized retail** displacing unorganized in India
- **Urban consumption** density plays
- **Banking franchises** with conservative lending (selective stakes)
- **Long India** without macro trading

## How he makes money

- **Compounding ROCE** in operating business (operating + investing)
- **Reinvestment moat** — each store adds network density
- **Time arbitrage** — willing to wait years for payoff
- **Buying stakes** when others fearful (contrarian but selective)

## What Damani avoids

- **Complex conglomerates** without clarity
- **Promotional management** seeking limelight
- **Businesses requiring constant capital** without returns
- **Frequent trading** and leverage

## NSE scuttlebutt (Damani-style)

- Store footfall, basket size, new city rollout pace
- Inventory turns vs peers
- Lease vs owned property strategy
- Promoter simplicity and capital allocation history
