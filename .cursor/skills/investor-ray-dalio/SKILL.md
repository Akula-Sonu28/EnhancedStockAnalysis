# Ray Dalio — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

Understand the **economic machine** — productivity growth + short-term debt cycle + long-term debt cycle. **Diversify** across environments that behave differently. Systemize decisions; stress-test beliefs.

## The economic machine (simplified)

```
Productivity ↑ (slow, stable)
+ Short-term cycle (5–8 yrs): credit expansion/contraction
+ Long-term debt cycle (50–75 yrs): deleveraging / restructuring
= Growth, inflation, policy response
```

## How he chooses investments (Bridgewater style)

### Step 1 — Identify regime
| Regime | Growth | Inflation | Favors (roughly) |
|--------|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Goldilocks | ↑ | Stable/low | Equities, credit |
| Reflation | ↑ | ↑ | Commodities, inflation-linked |
| Stagflation | ↓ | ↑ | Commodities, gold; hurt equities |
| Deflation | ↓ | ↓ | Bonds, cash; hurt risk assets |

### Step 2 — Balance sheet mechanics
- Who is **long assets / short liabilities**?
- Deleveraging = painful adjustment (austerity, defaults, money printing)

### Step 3 — Portfolio construction (All Weather concept)
Hold assets that perform in **different** environments — not bet on one forecast:
- Equities (growth)
- Nominal bonds (deflation hedge)
- Inflation-linked bonds (inflation hedge)
- Commodities (inflation spike)
- Diversifiers / risk parity balance

### Step 4 — Single stocks (secondary for Dalio)
Macro dominates; equities chosen for **exposure to themes** fitting regime (e.g. inflation beneficiaries).

## Trends Dalio tracks

- **Central bank policy** and yield curve
- **Credit spreads**, liquidity
- **Geopolitical conflict** and supply chains
- **Currency regimes** (USD, INR for India exposure)
- **Populism / wealth inequality** → policy shifts

## How he makes money

- **Macro bets** when consensus wrong (size matters)
- **Risk parity** — balanced risk contributions, not capital weights
- **Process edge** — systematic rules, debate culture
- **Crisis alpha** — liquidity when others forced to sell

## Principles for decisions

- **Radical open-mindedness** — stress-test thesis
- **Believability-weighted** debate
- **Pain + reflection = progress**
- **Don't confuse what you wish with what is**

## India application

- RBI cycle vs Fed → FPI flows, INR, rate-sensitive banks/NBFCs
- Fiscal + infrastructure capex phase → cyclicals vs defensives
- Not a stock-picker skill — **tilt sector allocation** by regime
