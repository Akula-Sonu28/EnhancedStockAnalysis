# Peter Lynch — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

**Know what you own.** Individual investors can beat institutions by spotting growth in everyday life before Wall Street. Buy **earnings growth at a reasonable price (GARP)** — PEG ≈ 1 or below is attractive.

## Six categories (classify every stock)

| Category | Traits | Lynch approach |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Slow growers** | Mature, dividends | Income, not big gains |
| **Stalwarts** | Large, 10–12% growth | Defensive growth |
| **Fast growers** | Small/mid, 20–25%+ | Best multibaggers — watch deceleration |
| **Cyclicals** | Earnings swing with economy | Trade cycles, don't hold through peak |
| **Turnarounds** | Near bankruptcy fixing | High risk/reward — verify recovery |
| **Asset plays** | Hidden assets | Unlock value |

**Rule:** Know which category — wrong playbook kills returns.

## How he chooses stocks

### Scuttlebutt (like Fisher, but simpler)
- Visit stores, count cars in parking lots, talk to suppliers
- **Local edge:** you see trends before analysts

### Quantitative checklist
- **PEG** = P/E ÷ earnings growth rate (< 1 favorable, > 2 caution)
- Debt reasonable vs equity
- Inventory not growing faster than sales
- Cash flow supports reported earnings
- Share count not exploding (dilution)

### Story must be simple
"If you can't explain why you own it in 2 minutes, don't own it."

## When to sell (Lynch)

- Story changed (competition caught up)
- Category misclassified (cyclical treated as grower)
- Fast grower becomes stalwart — lower expectations
- No reason to own vs better idea (not because price wiggled)

## Trends Lynch exploited

- **Consumer brands** visible in malls (Gap, Taco Bell era)
- **Bank turnarounds** when NPLs peak (cycle timing)
- **Niche industrials** growing faster than perceived
- **India parallel:** visible consumption, retail footfall, hospital chains, niche exporters

## How he makes money

- Many names (Fidelity Magellan) but **each idea has clear thesis**
- **Earnings surprises** drive re-rating
- **Multibaggers** from fast growers held while growth persists
- Avoids predicting market level — stock-picking focus

## Red flags

- Hot stock in hot industry (late)
- Whispers "new paradigm" without earnings
- Institutional darling at peak PEG
- Cyclical at top of cycle with peak earnings multiple

## NSE application

- Classify: is HDFC Bank stalwart vs cyclical fin?
- Check PEG on **consolidated** growth
- Use channel checks: app downloads, delivery volumes, management guidance vs reality
