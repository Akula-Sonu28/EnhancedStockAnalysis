# Benjamin Graham — Investment Framework

## Core philosophy

**Price ≠ value.** Buy with a **margin of safety** — gap between calculated intrinsic value and market price. Mr. Market offers daily quotes; you are not obligated to trade.

## Two investor types

| Type | Approach | Time |
|------|----------|------|
| **Defensive** | Diversified quality + minimum standards | Minimal effort |
| **Enterprising** | Special situations, net-nets, workouts | High effort |

## How he chooses stocks

### Defensive criteria (simplified modern adaptation)

- Adequate size and liquidity
- Strong financial condition (current ratio, debt)
- Earnings stability (no deficit in last 5–10 years)
- Dividend record
- Earnings growth (minimum over years)
- Moderate P/E vs history
- Moderate P/B

### Enterprising / deep value

| Screen | Formula / idea | Edge |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Net-net** | NCAV = current assets − total liabilities; buy below ⅔ NCAV | Liquidation value cushion |
| **Low P/E** | Earnings power cheap vs history | Mean reversion |
| **Asset plays** | Hidden assets, real estate, stakes | Market mislabels |
| **Special situations** | Merger arb, spinoffs, litigation outcomes | Event-driven |

### Intrinsic value methods

1. **Asset-based** — NCAV, book value adjustments
2. **Earnings power** — normalized EPS × conservative multiple
3. **Combination** — weigh both; require **margin of safety** (often 33%+ discount)

## Trends Graham rode

- **Post-Depression dislocation** — stocks below liquidation value
- **Cigar butts** — one last puff of value from dying companies (Buffett later moved away)
- **Relative cheapness** within diversified portfolio

## How he makes money

- **Statistical cheapness** across many names (enterprising)
- **Reversion** when market recognizes value
- **Corporate actions** unlock value (buyouts, liquidations)
- **Discipline** — rules remove emotion

## What Graham avoids

- Speculation without valuation anchor
- Growth stories without measurable safety
- Illiquid microcaps for defensive investor
- Ignoring balance sheet in favor of narrative

## Modern / NSE adaptation

- True net-nets rare on NSE; use **adjusted NCAV**, cash-rich smallcaps cautiously
- Watch **promoter pledging** and related-party drains — not in original Graham era
- **Ind AS** reclassifications affect book; normalize carefully
- Defensive screen → large liquid names with solid balance sheets

- "In the short run, the market is a voting machine; in the long run, a weighing machine."
