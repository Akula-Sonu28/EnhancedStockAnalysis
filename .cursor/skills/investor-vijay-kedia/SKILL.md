# Vijay Kedia — Investment Framework (India)

## Core philosophy

Find **SMILE** companies — small today, **large opportunity tomorrow**, with **entrepreneurial management** you trust. Hold through volatility if thesis intact. "Invest in businesses, not stocks."

## SMILE framework

| Letter | Meaning | What to verify |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| **S** | Small | Market cap room to grow (not mega-cap) |
| **M** | Medium | Management — integrity, passion, competence |
| **I** | Integrity | Promoter honesty, minority treatment |
| **L** | Large | Large opportunity — TAM, export, category creation |
| **E** | Extraordinary | Extraordinary growth potential in earnings |

*(Kedia also uses "Medium" for manageable debt and sensible scale — versions vary; emphasize Management + Integrity.)*

## How he chooses stocks

1. **Entrepreneur interview** — promoter vision, past delivery
2. **Scalable niche** — global or domestic leader in narrow segment
3. **Balance sheet** — survive downturns (Medium debt)
4. **Early inflection** — before full market recognition
5. **Hold 5–10+ years** if SMILE intact

## Sectors Kedia favored (patterns)

- **Specialty chemicals**, auto ancillaries, engineering exports
- **Pharma/API** niche players
- **Consumer brands** emerging from regional to national
- **Avoid:** commodity without differentiation, promoter red flags

## How he makes money

- **Multibaggers** from small → mid → large cap migration
- **Low turnover** — taxes and mistakes minimized
- **Concentrated** high-conviction portfolio (public talks: few names)
- **Learning from mistakes** — exits when integrity or growth breaks

## Red flags (Kedia)

- Promoter **pledge**, related-party looting
- **Growth by acquisition** without integration skill
- **Capex** without ROCE improvement
- Chasing **hot sectors** without SMILE fit

## Liquidity warning (NSE small/mid)

- Position size vs **ADV** — SMILE names often illiquid
- Corporate governance risk higher in smallcap India
