📖 Every Term Explained in Plain English

Overall stock quality. Considers fundamentals, momentum and value. 80+ is excellent.
FUND_SCORE 0–100
Fundamentals score — PE ratio, ROE, Debt levels, financial health.
MOM_SCORE 0–100
Momentum score — how strongly the stock has been trending recently.
VALUE_SCORE 0–100
Value score — is the stock cheap relative to earnings and assets?
RSI 0–100
Relative Strength Index. >70 = overbought (may fall). <30 = oversold (may rise). 40–65 = healthy zone.
SUPPORT ₹ price
Floor price where buyers step in strongly. Strong stocks bounce off support repeatedly.
RESISTANCE ₹ price
Ceiling price where sellers dominate. PRE-BREAKOUT = price is about to crack this ceiling.
STOP LOSS ₹ price
Your safety net. If price drops here — exit immediately. Always set a price alert in your broker.
52W RANGE BAR Visual
Blue dot on the slider = current price between 52-week low and high. Near left = cheap, near right = expensive.
BREAKOUT % 0–100%
Probability the stock breaks above resistance. 80%+ = high-confidence breakout setup.
BOOK % IF SELL Percent
Don't sell everything. Book this % and keep the rest riding. E.g., 50% = sell half.
ML SIGNAL BUY/SELL/HOLD
AI model prediction from 65 technical features. Confidence bar shows how sure the model is.
PE RATIO Number
Price-to-Earnings. Lower = cheaper stock. Under 12 for PSU banks is cheap. Over 35 is expensive.
ROE % Percent
Return on Equity. How efficiently the company uses shareholder money. 15%+ is healthy.
DEBT/EQUITY Ratio
0 for banks (normal). For others: under 1 is healthy, over 2 is risky.
VOLATILITY % Percent
Daily price swing range. 15–25% = moderate. Over 40% = high risk / high reward.
20D CHANGE % Percent
Price change over last 20 trading days (~1 month). Shows recent momentum.
SWAP TARGET Symbol
Sell the current stock and immediately buy this target. Money moves to a better-ranked opportunity.
EXHAUSTION Flag
Recent rally is running out of steam. Buyers are tired. Avoid adding more right now.
SENT_ADJ +/- pts
Sentiment score adjustment — positive if news/social mood is bullish on this stock.
PATTERN_ADJ +/- pts
Chart pattern bonus. Recognises 15+ patterns like head-and-shoulders, flags, triangles.
